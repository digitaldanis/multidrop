Send files from an iPhone to a PC without a cable

Photos, videos, PDFs – straight from the iPhone to your Windows or Linux PC over Wi-Fi. No cable, no iTunes, no cloud upload.

Download for Windows Install on Linux ★ Source code on GitHub

On Linux: AirDrop

With a supported Wi-Fi card (MediaTek MT7925, Intel in exclusive mode), your Linux PC appears in the iPhone's AirDrop list. Tap Share → AirDrop → your PC. See AirDrop for Linux.

On Windows and Linux: LocalSend

Install the free LocalSend app on the iPhone. It finds MultiDrop on the PC automatically; pick the files and the PC.

PC → iPhone without any app

Share files from MultiDrop and scan the QR code with the iPhone camera. Safari asks for the PIN shown on the PC and downloads the files.

Questions

Is it private?

Yes. Transfers go directly over your network, encrypted where the protocol supports it; there is no server and no account.
