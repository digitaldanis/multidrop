Welcome to MultiDrop

Phones and computers nearby can now send files to this PC – and you can send to them.

  • Android or Samsung phone: tap Share → Quick Share and pick .
  • iPhone: use the free LocalSend app. “Download in the browser” below needs no app, but Safari on many iPhones refuses it on a home network.
  • From this PC: drag files onto a device below, or right-click files in Explorer → Send to → MultiDrop.

Windows asks once whether MultiDrop may make changes: choose Yes, so phones on your Wi-Fi can reach this PC. Closing this window keeps MultiDrop running in the notification area, so you can still receive.
